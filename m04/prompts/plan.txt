- open_ticket: an item arrived damaged, broken, missing or wrong
- manual_search: how to use or fix a product, an error code, warranty or returns rules (product manuals)
- answer: anything else

H200 (headset), D300 (dock), M270 (monitor), FAQ (warranty, returns, refunds); otherwise "".
