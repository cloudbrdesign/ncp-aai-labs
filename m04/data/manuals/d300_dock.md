# D300 USB-C Dock: user manual

Product code: D300
Revision: 2026-05 (firmware 2.4)

## Overview

The D300 turns one USB-C port on your laptop into a desk setup. Ports: two HDMI 2.0
outputs, one DisplayPort 1.4 output, three USB-A 3.2 ports, one USB-C data port, a
gigabit Ethernet port and an SD card reader. The included 130 W power adapter charges
your laptop through the dock with up to 100 W.

Printed copy: check the online manual for the latest version.

## Setup

Connect the power adapter to the dock first, then connect the dock's USB-C cable to your
laptop. Use the cable that came with the dock: it is rated for 100 W and 10 Gbit/s, and
many phone cables are not. The status LED on the front glows white when the dock works
normally. Windows and macOS need no driver for the basic ports.

## Connecting displays

The D300 drives up to three external displays: two over HDMI and one over DisplayPort.
Your laptop's USB-C port must support DisplayPort Alt Mode. To use two or three
different pictures (extended desktop), the port must also support Multi-Stream Transport
(MST). If your laptop has no MST support, the second display shows the same picture as
the first (mirroring) or stays blank. Resolution: one display at 4K 60 Hz, or two
displays at 4K 30 Hz, or three at 1080p 60 Hz.

## Troubleshooting

The status LED blinks orange when the dock finds a problem; the D300 Dock Utility shows
the error code.

Error E42: the power adapter gives too little power. This happens with adapters below
65 W. Use the 130 W adapter that came with the dock and connect it before the laptop.

Error E45: the dock is too hot. Keep the vents on both sides free and do not stack
anything on the dock. It works again after it cools down.

Error E47: a ﬁrmware update failed. Keep the dock connected to power and run the update
again from the D300 Dock Utility.

Ethernet does not connect: check the cable, then unplug the dock from power for 10
seconds. The link LED on the Ethernet port glows green when the connection works.

## Firmware updates

Install the D300 Dock Utility from the support page, connect the dock, and choose Check
for updates. The update takes about 3 minutes; do not unplug the dock while the LED
blinks white. The current ﬁrmware is 2.4, which fixes screen flicker at 4K 60 Hz.

## Specifications

- Host connection: USB-C with DisplayPort Alt Mode, 10 Gbit/s
- Laptop charging: up to 100 W (with the 130 W adapter)
- Video: 2x HDMI 2.0, 1x DisplayPort 1.4, MST for extended displays
- Size: 190 x 75 x 25 mm, weight 420 g
