# H200 Wireless Headset: user manual

Product code: H200
Revision: 2026-01 (firmware 1.2)

## In the box

The H200 is an over-ear wireless headset with active noise cancelling (ANC) and a
fold-away boom microphone. In the box you find the headset, a USB-C charging cable,
a 3.5 mm audio cable for wired listening, and a soft travel case.

Printed copy: check the online manual for the latest version.

## Charging

Charge the headset fully before first use. Connect the USB-C cable to the port on the
right ear cup and to any USB charger of 5 V. The LED glows red while charging and turns
green when the battery is full, after about 2 hours. Ten minutes of charging gives about
3 hours of listening. If the LED does not light up at all, try another cable and charger.

## Pairing

To pair a new phone or laptop, switch the headset off, then hold the power button for
5 seconds until the LED flashes blue and red. On your device, open the Bluetooth
settings and choose "H200". The headset remembers up to 6 devices and can stay
connected to two of them at the same time (multipoint). To connect a second device,
repeat the pairing steps while the first device stays connected.

## Controls

Press the power button once to play or pause, twice to skip to the next track. The + and
- buttons change the volume. Hold the ANC button for 2 seconds to switch between noise
cancelling, transparency mode and off. Folding the boom microphone up mutes it; a short
tone tells you the microphone is muted.

## Troubleshooting

No sound: check that the headset is selected as the audio output on your device and that
the volume is not at zero. Crackling or dropouts: stay within 10 metres of the device and
away from microwave ovens and busy Wi-Fi routers.

Error E21 (a voice prompt says "error twenty-one"): the battery is too cold or too hot to
charge. Let the headset reach room temperature, between 10 and 35 °C, then charge again.

Error E24: the pairing list is full. Reset the headset to clear it: hold the + and -
buttons together for 10 seconds until the LED flashes purple. After a reset, pair your
devices again.

## Specifications

- Bluetooth 5.3, codecs SBC and AAC, range up to 10 m
- Battery: up to 30 hours with ANC off, 22 hours with ANC on
- Charging: USB-C, full charge in about 2 hours
- Weight: 250 g
- Firmware: 1.2 (update with the H200 Companion app)
