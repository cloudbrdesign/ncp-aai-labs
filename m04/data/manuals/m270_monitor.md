# M270 27-inch 4K Monitor: user manual

Product code: M270
Revision: 2026-04

## In the box

The M270 is a 27-inch 4K (3840 x 2160) IPS monitor with a height-adjustable stand. In the
box you find the monitor, the stand, a power cable, an HDMI cable and a USB-C cable.

Printed copy: check the online manual for the latest version.

## Setup

Attach the stand to the back of the monitor until it clicks. To mount the monitor on a
wall arm instead, remove the stand and use the VESA 100 x 100 mm holes with M4 screws.
The stand tilts, swivels and raises the screen by up to 130 mm.

## Connecting

Connect a laptop with one USB-C cable: it carries the picture and charges the laptop
with up to 65 W. You can also use HDMI 2.0 or DisplayPort 1.4. For 4K at 60 Hz over
HDMI, use a High Speed HDMI cable; older cables limit the picture to 30 Hz. The monitor
picks the active input by itself; to choose one by hand, open the on-screen menu.

## On-screen menu

Press the joystick under the bottom edge to open the on-screen menu. Move it up and down
to choose an item and press it to confirm. Picture modes: Standard, Reading (less blue
light) and sRGB. Brightness and contrast are under Picture. The monitor switches off
the screen after 10 minutes without a signal to save power.

## Troubleshooting

Error Ｅ７３ on screen: there is no signal on the selected input. Check that the cable
is firmly connected at both ends, wake the computer, and choose the right input in the
on-screen menu.

Error E76: the USB-C port is overloaded. The laptop asks for more than 65 W. Connect the
laptop's own charger as well, or use HDMI or DisplayPort for the picture.

The screen flickers: turn off Adaptive Sync in the on-screen menu under Picture, and try
another cable. Dead pixels: up to two dead pixels are within the product specification.

## Specifications

- Panel: 27-inch IPS, 3840 x 2160, 60 Hz, 350 cd/m2
- Inputs: USB-C (DisplayPort Alt Mode, 65 W charging), HDMI 2.0, DisplayPort 1.4
- Stand: tilt, swivel, height 130 mm; VESA 100 x 100
- Power use: 30 W typical, under 0.5 W on standby
