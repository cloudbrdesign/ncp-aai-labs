# Warranty and returns: questions and answers

Product code: FAQ
Revision: 2026-06

## Warranty

Every product we sell has a 2-year warranty from the delivery date. Cables, cases and
other accessories have a 1-year warranty. The warranty covers faults in materials and
workmanship. It does not cover damage from drops, liquids, or repairs by someone else.

## Warranty claims

To make a warranty claim, open a support ticket with your order ID, the product's serial
number (on the label under the product) and a short description of the fault. We reply
within 2 business days and send a prepaid shipping label if the product has to come back.
Repaired or replaced products keep the rest of the original warranty.

Printed copy: check the online manual for the latest version.

## Returns

You can return items within 30 days of delivery. Items must be unused and in their
original packaging. Orders that have not been delivered yet cannot be returned; you can
cancel them instead. To start a return, send us your order ID.

## Refunds

Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 business days of the return
arriving at our warehouse. Shipping costs are refunded when the item was faulty or we
sent the wrong item.

## Damaged in transit

If an item arrives damaged, tell us within 48 hours of delivery and send a photo of the
item and the box. We open a ticket and send a replacement or a refund, whichever you
prefer. Please keep the packaging until the ticket is closed.
